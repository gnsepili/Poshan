Poshan AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Poshan AI ("we", "us") is an AI health and nutrition coach app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. Published at https://gnsepili.github.io/Poshan/privacy/.

Data we collect

  • Account: your email address, and your password if you sign up with email (stored hashed by our authentication provider). If you choose Sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture. We never receive your Google password or any other Google account data.
  • Health profile and goals: age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and the targets you set (weight, calories, macros, steps).
  • Meals: descriptions, nutrition estimates (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre) and meal photos you capture.
  • Body composition: photos of InBody (or similar) result sheets you upload and the values read from them.
  • Activity: workouts and steps you log. If you connect Health Connect (Android), steps, active calories, heart rate and exercise sessions — only after you grant permission.
  • Coach conversations: messages you exchange with the AI coach, plus the meal and workout plans it creates for you.
  • Notifications: a push-notification token for your device, if you allow notifications.
  • Diagnostics: error reports and basic in-app usage events, stored in our own database (no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs).

Some data (recent screens and meals you log while offline) is also cached on your device so the app works without a connection. It is removed when you sign out.

How we use it

  • To provide the coaching features: estimating meal nutrition, reading body-composition scans, daily targets and coach notes, meal and workout plans, and chat.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To send the notifications you opted into (e.g. your morning coach note).
  • To find and fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles for anyone else.

Who processes it

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app, each processing it on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage (servers in Singapore).
  • OpenAI — the AI models that analyse your meal and InBody photos and power the coach. Data sent through OpenAI's API is not used by OpenAI to train its models.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering push notifications.
  • Google — only if you choose Sign in with Google.

We may disclose data if required by law.

Google user data

Poshan AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google account data is used only to sign you in.

Health Connect

Poshan AI reads Steps, Active Calories, Heart Rate and Exercise Sessions from Health Connect only after you grant permission, and uses them solely to show and coach on your activity in the app. We do not sell Health Connect data or use it for advertising. You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect settings.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. To delete your account and all associated data (profile, meals, photos, scans, activity, conversations), email us at the address below; we complete deletion within 30 days, including the error logs linked to your account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each user's records and photos are isolated with database row-level security, and InBody photos are stored in a private bucket.

Children

Poshan AI is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Not medical advice

Poshan AI provides general wellness guidance. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a doctor or dietitian.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the "Last updated" date above.
